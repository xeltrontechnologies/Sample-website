Position lifecycle

Three states, start to finish

Deposited

Funds committed

Your deposit is allocated to the connected market position at the day's rate. A maturity date is set immediately.

Maturing

Tracking the market

The position moves with the underlying asset. Value shown is marked to market, not a fixed promise.

Unlocked

Withdraw anytime

Once the term ends, the position closes and the balance — gain, loss, or flat — is available to withdraw.
